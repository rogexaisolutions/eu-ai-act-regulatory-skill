# RR-02 corrective-action basis review

## Classification: NO ISSUE

**Global mandatory assertion:** “Separate legal, potential legal, engineering and project bases; verified law includes required metadata.” The current skill asks to “Classify every material requirement/recommendation independently of its priority” and keep each item’s origin traceable. The approved functional specification likewise links corrective actions to the obligation and evidence IDs. Neither requires a literal `ENGINEERING RECOMMENDATION` label or a dedicated basis column where the basis is clear from context.

The response separates the fictional source from proposed work. It labels the source as “**EXERCISE-STATED, UNVERIFIED**,” describes F-1 as a “**Hypothetical exercise obligation**,” and confines the assessment to the stipulated exercise. The two actions appear under “**Proportionate corrective actions**,” each links to F-1, and the response says “**Actions are PLANNED**” and that priority reflects the gap “**under the exercise assumption**.” The first action proposes an implementation change; the second proposes a test and evidence record. Those links and labels make the actions’ basis and origin sufficiently clear as proposed remediation for the hypothetical exercise. They are not presented as verified real-world legal duties or as project requirements.

Adding the literal basis label could improve uniformity, but requiring it here would impose a formatting rule beyond the existing contract. No skill correction is warranted.
