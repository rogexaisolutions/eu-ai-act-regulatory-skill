# REGULATORY REVIEW

Apply the shared basis, source, evidence, scope and escalation rules in SKILL.md. This optional capability assesses proposed and implemented AI systems; depth follows requested scope and available evidence. Reuse [sources.md](sources.md), [triage.md](triage.md) and [controls.md](controls.md) as relevant.

- Establish reviewed system/version, scope, evidence cutoff, intended purpose, jurisdiction, affected people, lifecycle, data/model flows, autonomy and actor roles. Separate facts, inspected evidence, reports, assumptions and unknowns.
- Screen applicable EU AI Act scope, prohibited practices, high-risk routes/exceptions, actor-specific duties and application timing; consider GDPR and sector law when triggered. Do not classify from labels, human oversight or licensing alone. Escalate material uncertainty and high-impact uses.
- Verify official sources using the shared source rules. Record act/article, jurisdiction, URI, authentic text/version, verification date, entry into force, relevant application date, actor and applicability rationale. If source currency or applicability remains unresolved, retain POTENTIAL LEGAL OBLIGATION and LEGAL SOURCE NOT VERIFIED as appropriate; continue useful provisional work. For a fictional or hypothetical legal instrument, preserve any supplied URI, version, stated verification date, application date and actor only as **EXERCISE-STATED, UNVERIFIED** information. Explicitly state that these details are not an authentic or verified legal source and do not establish real-world applicability; never omit or present them as independently verified.
- For each obligation, map supplied/inspected evidence with stable ID, revision/date, provenance, supported scope and execution status. Judge relevance, authenticity, coverage and consistency for that actor/system/period. Explain sufficiency; expose conflicting records. Reports and plans are not execution evidence.
- Separate applicability (applicable, unresolved, or NOT APPLICABLE with rationale), evidence sufficiency and assessment result. Missing evidence is NOT VERIFIED, never confirmed non-compliance. An unresolved applicability question needs facts/source interpretation, not an assumed violation.
- A confirmed non-compliance finding requires a verified applicable legal obligation and sufficient contradictory evidence of the specific unmet requirement. Explicitly mark every REGULATORY REVIEW conclusion **SUBJECT TO QUALIFIED LEGAL REVIEW**, including hypothetical exercise findings, documented non-compliance findings and favorable no-material-gap conclusions. Keep the conclusion scoped; this label does not authenticate fictional sources or imply formal adjudication. Link any non-compliance finding to the obligation and contrary evidence. Separate optional engineering improvements and project requirements using their existing bases.
- Link corrective actions to findings/obligations, resolution evidence, dependencies, priority and known/proposed owner or UNASSIGNED. Work remains PLANNED until evidenced. No-gap items need no invented corrective work.
- Conclude proportionately: sufficient evidence can support “no material gap identified within this reviewed scope and evidence cutoff,” with residual limitations. Do not demand perfection, invent duties or report compliance percentages. No certification, formal conformity assessment, legal approval, deployment authorization or residual-risk acceptance.

Reuse existing requirement/control/test/evidence IDs and wording; assign new IDs only to new records. Hand corrective design work to DESIGN/IMPROVE and requested records to DOCUMENT without duplicating their outputs or requiring a full review for ordinary tasks. Proposals receive applicability/planned-evidence review; implementation claims require relevant evidence. Plain structured Markdown is sufficient; unavailable tools trigger explicit limits, not private-service dependencies.
