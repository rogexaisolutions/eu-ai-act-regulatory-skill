## Core provision index (AI Act)
- Article 2: scope, exclusions, conditional free/open-source treatment.
- Article 3: definitions and actors.
- Article 4: AI literacy obligations.
- Article 5: prohibited practices; amendments may alter timing of specific prohibitions.
- Article 6 and Annexes I/III: high-risk routes; exceptions require case-specific demonstration.
- Articles 8–15: substantive requirements for high-risk systems.
- Articles 16–27: duties along value chain, as applicable; Article 25 role changes.
- Article 43: conformity assessment procedures for applicable high-risk systems.
- Article 47 and Annex V: EU declaration of conformity when legally required; **not** the ROGEX Assurance Card template.
- Article 50: certain transparency obligations.
- Article 53: duties for general-purpose AI model providers; **not all users of GPAI models**.
- Article 113 and amendments: application dates; never reuse old schedules without rechecking.

## Freshness rules
1. Inspect the current consolidated legislation and its 'amended by' list.
2. Record date, URI/version, affected articles and whether the rule is applicable **now** or in the future.
3. Cross-check the Official Journal legal acts for legally authentic text.
4. If offline, report `LEGAL SOURCE NOT VERIFIED` and make recommendations provisional.
5. Do not copy comprehensive legal texts into the skill. Link the original sources and quote sparingly.

## Claim records and authority

For a verified LEGAL OBLIGATION record official source/provision, jurisdiction, text/version, verification date, relevant application date, actor and applicability rationale. Distinguish entry into force from application. Use POTENTIAL LEGAL OBLIGATION if applicability or currency is unresolved, recording missing facts, candidate source if available and a resolution action. A supplied index entry is not a verified legal claim.

Label guidance, standards and proposals by their authority/status; citation alone does not make them binding. Record voluntary engineering implementations separately from any legal objective. If reliable sources conflict, identify the conflict and seek human interpretation for material issues; do not silently select convenient text. Continue useful engineering work provisionally when verification is unavailable.
