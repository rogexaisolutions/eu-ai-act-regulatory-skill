- RR-07: Article 6(2)-(3), Annex III 4(a), actor definitions and Article 25. Recruitment scoring/filtering is a classification candidate; exception analysis must consider material influence and profiling. Human oversight alone is insufficient.
- RR-08: Article 16 links provider duties to Section 2; Articles 9-15 and 17 concern risks, data, documentation, records, transparency, oversight, performance and quality management. Article 11 requires documentation before market/service and updating. Lack of a supplied record is not proof of an operational violation. Pre-market lab status and planned first release matter.
- RR-09: Article 5(1)(f) addresses workplace/education emotion inference, with medical/safety exceptions; Article 3(39) connects emotion recognition to biometric data. Technical facts and actual purpose must be checked. Consent is not itself an exception stated in this provision.

Article 113 as amended sets Chapter III Sections 1-3 (except Article 6(5)) applicability for Article 6(2)/Annex III systems at 2 December 2027. Thus RR-07/08 use planned first release in January 2028, assessed in November 2026; they test readiness and future duties, not automatic current breaches. Chapter II generally applied from 2 February 2025; later exceptions for newly amended prohibitions must not be confused with Article 5(1)(f). Article 111 transition and Article 2 development exclusions require use-specific review.

Open qualified-review questions: profiling characterization and Article 6(3) exception eligibility; exact lab/development boundaries and completeness of future provider requirements; biometric feature characterization and genuine medical/safety purpose.
